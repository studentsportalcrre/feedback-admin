STUDENT FEEDBACK QUESTIONNAIRE

1]	Punctuality and regularity: The Teacher consistently starts and ends class on time and adheres to the planned schedule.
2]	Ability to engage students: The Teacher's ability to inspire, motivate and actively involve students in the learning process. 
3]	Knowledge of subject and preparation: The Teacher's expertise in the subject and their readiness for class sessions.
4]	Communication Skills: Communication skills of the Teacher while taking the class.
5]	 Use of teaching aids / technology: The Teacher has effectively used teaching aids, technology, Live examples, Case Study and learning resources.
6]	Encouragement of questions and discussion: The Teacher has created an interactive, dynamic, and safe learning environment.
7]	Completeness of Syllabus on time: The Teacher completes the syllabus on time for the semester. 
8]	Providing Course Material: The course material is adequately made available by the Teacher.
9]	Motivation and inspiration: The Teacher inspires/motivates the students to learn the Course.
10]	Overall learning environment: The Teacher has improved my understanding of the subject.
